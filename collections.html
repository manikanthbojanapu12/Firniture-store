Style Guide

The Art of Curated Living

Creating a premium home is about balancing forms, timber grains, and textiles. Our designers have compiled styling rules to help you mix and match collections with confidence.

Rule of Contrast

Pair low-profile seating with floating shelving and architectural lighting so spaces feel grounded and luminous.

Textural Balance

Combine rigid surfaces (like Carrara marble coffee tables) with rich, soft textiles (like Belgian bouclé or wool cushions) to balance industrial lines with organic warmth.
