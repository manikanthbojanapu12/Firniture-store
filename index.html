Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about buying handcrafted furniture with STACKLY

How does white-glove delivery and assembly work? +
We provide complimentary white-glove delivery for all large furniture pieces. Our dedicated logistics team brings your order directly into your room of choice, inspects, fully assembles each piece, and removes all packaging materials.
Are all pieces made from sustainable solid hardwood? +
Absolutely! Every piece in our collection is handcrafted from 100% FSC-certified solid hardwoods such as European white oak, American walnut, and kiln-dried teak. Every piece is covered by our comprehensive 10-year structural warranty.
What financing and payment options are available? +
We offer 0% APR financing for 6, 12, and 24 months on orders above $500 through top financing partners. We also support all major credit cards, Apple Pay, PayPal, and bank transfers with full buyer protection.
What is your 30-day in-home trial policy? +
We believe you need to experience furniture in your natural lighting and living space. If you're not completely in love within 30 days, we will arrange a white-glove pickup from your room and issue a 100% full refund.
Can I customize timber finishes or upholstery fabric? +
Yes! We offer tailored custom fabric choices, leather grades, and timber wood stains on our sofas, dining chairs, and bed frames. Contact our design concierge team or order a complimentary fabric swatch box to your home.
How do I care for solid wood and natural textiles? +
Each order includes a bespoke STACKLY Care Kit containing natural beeswax balm and organic fabric conditioner. We also provide step-by-step timber maintenance guides on our journal to keep your pieces looking pristine for decades.
